      Names on the plan, with grid square
      • Roads, plot outlines, colours, names and marks are traced from the revised plan of 16-10-2026. Nothing has been moved or resized.
      • One road is left out: the short link east of Mohammedi Apartment, which doesn't exist on site. Directions never use it.
      • Q, Z and M are shown exactly as printed on the plan. A boxed mark is a plot the plan marks BUILDING, with how many, for example Q × 2.
      • Green-yellow plots are a colour added in this revision. The plan's own legend doesn't name it, so it is listed on its own.
      • A building with a saved floor count stands to that height. Other block heights are only for presentation: apartments stand tallest, plots marked BUILDING a little lower, and named grounds and parking stay flat. The plan gives no measurements.
      • Plots drawn without a category colour stay neutral.
      • Building details are shared with everyone who can open this map while signed in to claude.ai. Only people with edit access can add or change them.
      • Grid squares A–H and 1–7 match the printed 2D and 3D sheets, so a place has the same reference everywhere. They are not measurements.
      • Directions follow the roads drawn on the plan. The plan has no scale, so the way is shown as a route with turns, not in metres.
      • Trees on the edge of the board are decoration. No trees or streetlights are placed inside the plan because the source does not show any.
