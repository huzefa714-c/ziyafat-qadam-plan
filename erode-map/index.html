      SourcesPoints and order: the shared Google Maps link · Roads, water, parks, rail and mapped buildings: OpenStreetMap · Route line: OSRM over OpenStreetMap roads · Satellite photos: Esri
      • Every point is at the exact place given in the shared Google Maps link, in the same order. Sri Thindal Cafe is the start point that guides people onto the route; the stops begin at Burhani Palace.
      • Roads, junctions and distances are to scale. The route follows OpenStreetMap roads; Google Maps may choose slightly different roads, so follow Google Maps while driving.
      • Each coloured block marks the stop's building, placed on its point and kept clear of the road. Its size and height show the stop, not the real building.
      • Few Erode buildings are mapped online. The white blocks along the route are drawn from the street layout to show the built-up area; their exact shapes and heights are not known. Blue-grey blocks are buildings mapped in OpenStreetMap.
      • Building details are shared with everyone who can open this team map while signed in to claude.ai. Only people with edit access can add or change them. A building with a saved floor count stands to that height.
      • Trees on the board edge are decoration. Street trees are illustrative.
      • Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Satellite imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics. Labels in satellite view: OpenFreeMap.
