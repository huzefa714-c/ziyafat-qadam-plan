      Stops, with grid square
      • The start point and the nine stops, and their order, are exactly as in the shared Google Maps link, at the link's coordinates. Sri Thindal Cafe is the start point that guides people onto the route; the stops begin at Burhani Palace.
      • The route follows OpenStreetMap roads through those points. Google's own route line can't be exported, so small differences from what Google Maps shows are possible.
      • Positions and the 1 km grid are to scale. Main roads are drawn about two and a half times wider than real so they read on the board; streets are printed flat.
      • The coloured stop blocks are markers placed beside the road, about 40–60 m from each exact point. Their size and height are not the real building. A block with a saved floor count stands taller.
      • White blocks are buildings mapped in OpenStreetMap, at their real size. Few buildings in Erode are mapped, and ones under the widened roads are left out.
      • Building details are shared with everyone who can open this map while signed in to claude.ai. Only people with edit access can add or change them.
      • Trees on the edge of the board are decoration.
